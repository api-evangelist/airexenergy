# Airex Energy

> Airex Energy (Airex Énergie) is a Laval, Québec cleantech manufacturer that converts
> residual biomass — forest residues, sawdust, bark and recycled wood — into biocoal,
> biochar and biocarbon with its patented CarbonFX torrefaction technology. It is an
> industrial company, not a software vendor: as of 2026-09-19 it publishes no API,
> developer portal, SDK, webhook surface or machine-readable contract.
